# Allow staking with U$D instead of NMR

---

### Post #1 — **rigrog** | 2021-08-03 18:29 UTC

NMR is _so_ 21st century! And _such_ a pain in the ass.

For all the price volatility, all the complication and “gas cost” involved in the round trip from USD to NMR to USD, what do we gain? Only one thing: the ethereum contract to _burn_ some of the NMR. Please excuse my lack of enthusiasm.

It’d be so much simpler and cheaper for all of us, if we could stake by just sending USD to Numerai. And when our models have a bad round: Numerai can _keep_ a few USD (compensating somewhat for trading losses), instead of the performative griefing of burning some NMR ( to _noone’s_ benefit!).
