I have these models: [kayeff_random](<https://numer.ai/kayeff_random/submissions>) & [modnar_ffeyak](<https://numer.ai/modnar_ffeyak/submissions>). Please only look at the rounds beginning at round 370, because at earlier rounds the latter model submitted incorrect 1-p predictions.
