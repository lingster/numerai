### Post #2 — **kayeffnumeraitor** | 2022-07-21 20:23 UTC

Who knows? But to be honest, I don’t think it will reach 90$ in any short timeframe. However, if crypto enters a bull market again or interest in Numerai goes viral for whatever reason, it might happen, but probably for a very short time.  
Keep in mind that on the rather long timescale (many years?) the fate of NMR is not secured, as there is a fixed amount of NMR that can run out, forcing Numerai to either buy back some NMR (which is good for us) or cancels the tournament (which is bad). Or, while it does not seem to be the case for now, if Numerai goes bankrupt or cancels the tournament for whatever reason, NMR price will become essentially 0.

### Post #10 — **kayeffnumeraitor** | 2022-08-27 13:40 UTC _(reply to #9)_

Depends on what your goal is. If you want to be part of the prediction game and want a little bit more security (especially if you stake considerable amounts of money), there is the possibility to short sell NMR: If NMRs drop to 0 you wont suffer a total loss. Obviously your models then have to be good enough so that the earnings cover the cost of the NMR short positions, so that you have a net win in the end.

If your goal is just to speculate on NMR being more worth in a few years, you’ll have to accept the risk or don’t buy it. I think the risk is somewhat comparable to investing in stocks of a company in its early stages.
