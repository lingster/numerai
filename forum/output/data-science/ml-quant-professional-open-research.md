# ML-Quant: Professional & Open Research

---

### Post #1 — **snowdere** | 2022-01-12 21:57 UTC

A couple of months ago I helped launch the Kaggle G-Research crypto competition, I have since thought about methods to obtain up-to-date research that could be used by participants to discover signals.

The solution was [ML-Quant](<https://www.ml-quant.com/>), a public and free solution that will get you halfway there. So far the focus has been on academic literature (like ArXiv, SSRN, and RePec), and general media (Blogs, Podcasts, Videos).

A few Numerai members have responded to my LinkedIn post, so I thought I would post it here for your feedback and recommendations.

And would happily answer any question too!
