# Huge thanks to Josef, and a call to action for the community!

---

### Post #1 — **ia_ai** | 2024-05-02 15:11 UTC

We want to give **a big shoutout to Josef** ([@svendaj](</u/svendaj>)) for his fantastic work creating and maintaining [his Kaggle notebooks for Numerai](<https://www.kaggle.com/svendaj/code?query=numerai>)!

Josef’s notebooks have been a huge asset to the community, and we know they’ve helped people get started or improve their approaches.

In order to show our appreciation for Josef’s efforts, we’ve sent him **a bounty of 50 NMR**. We hope this encourages him to keep up the great work ![:pray:](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/twitter/pray.png?v=13)

**This is also a call to action for all of you in the community!**

We’re always looking for high-quality contributions that can benefit everyone. Whether it’s creating new notebooks, improving existing ones, or sharing insightful discussions, we value your participation. So don’t be shy - share your knowledge and help us all grow together!

Here are some ways you can contribute:

  * Create new Kaggle notebooks that tackle interesting problems in the Numerai dataset.
  * Improve existing notebooks by adding new functionalities, fixing errors, or making them more user-friendly.
  * Share you insights and learnings in the forum discussions.



Let’s keep this community a vibrant hub for learning and collaboration!

Joe (on behalf of the Numerai Council of Elders)

**Bonus fact:**

Josef also kindly agreed to give a presentation about his notebooks at the recent Frankfurt meetup. Do check out the slides and videos at [our meetup repo](<https://github.com/councilofelders/meetups>).
