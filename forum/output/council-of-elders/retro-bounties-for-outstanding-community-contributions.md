# Retro Bounties for Outstanding Community Contributions

---

### Post #1 — **ia_ai** | 2026-02-03 17:59 UTC

The Council of Elders is excited to announce two **retro bounties** honouring exceptional contributions from our community members. These bounties celebrate thoughtful, high-quality work that has meaningfully advanced collective understanding and collaboration within the Numerai ecosystem.

  * **First Retro Bounty –[Ling Li](<https://x.com/Lingster888>)**  
For compiling detailed notes and documentation on the **Numerai project** , including in-depth coverage of the **GraphQL API**. This work has provided a consistent and valuable reference for both new and experienced participants.  
Check it out here: **<https://github.com/lingster/numerai>**

  * **Second Retro Bounty –** [* **Degerhan**](<https://x.com/DegerhanUsluel>)  
For the insightful forum post **“Vibesciencing my way through v5.2 data (Faith II)”** , where Degerhan shared a thoughtful and exploratory approach to understanding the latest dataset. This piece was even mentioned by **MikeP during NumerCon** , recognizing its creativity and analytical depth.  
Read the post here: **<https://forum.numer.ai/t/vibesciencing-my-way-through-v5-2-data-faith-ii/8214>**

  * **Third Retro Bounty – lostdev**  
For **being a consistently super helpful dude** on the Numerai Discord, generously answering questions, guiding newer users, and helping keep the conversation productive and welcoming. His contributions were even mentioned by multiple community members at NumerCon, highlighting the positive impact he has had across the ecosystem.

A big thank you to lostdev for embodying the collaborative spirit that makes this community special. As recognition, he will also receive a 50 NMR retro bounty, to be sent very soon.




A huge congratulations to both Lingster and Degerhan! Your work truly reflects the curiosity, generosity, and collaborative spirit that keep this community thriving. To show our appreciation, each of you will receive a **50 NMR** bounty, which will be sent very soon.
