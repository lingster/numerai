# [Governance Proposal] Abridged community review for small retroactive bounties

---

### Post #1 — **jrb** | 2021-07-29 15:43 UTC

I think we all agree that ceteris paribus, a faster CoE is a better CoE. With that in mind, I’d like to propose that we shorten the community review period for small retroactive bounties that the CoE awards down to 48 hours, subject to the following conditions:

  1. The proposal is written by a current member of the CoE
  2. The bounty is retroactive and no more than 10 NMR
  3. The recipient of the bounty is not a current member of the CoE



If all three of these conditions cannot be met, we should default back to a community review period of no less than 7 days.

Should the community review period for small retroactive bounties (<= 10 NMR) from the CoE be reduced to 48 hours?

  * Yes
  * No
