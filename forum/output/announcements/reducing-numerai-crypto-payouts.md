# Reducing Numerai Crypto Payouts

---

### Post #1 — **ark** | 2025-01-24 21:38 UTC

Hi all,

It has come to our attention that Numerai Crypto is far more profitable for users than our other 2 tournaments. While we have been happy to provide value to users, this is unsustainable and we will be drastically reducing the payout multipliers of Numerai Crypto.

The new Multipliers will be 0xCORR + 1xMMC to bring the profitability of Numerai Crypto more in-line with our other tournaments. This change will take effect immediately. All Numerai Crypto rounds starting on or after Saturday, Jan. 24, 2025 will use these multipliers.

Please see the attached charts to see how this affects payouts.
