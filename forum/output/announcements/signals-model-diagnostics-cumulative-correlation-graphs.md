# Signals Model Diagnostics: Cumulative Correlation Graphs

---

### Post #1 — **_liamhz** | 2021-03-18 19:09 UTC

Model diagnostics for new Signals submissions now include a graph showing your submission’s cumulative correlation on recent validation eras.  


[![image](https://canada1.discourse-cdn.com/flex009/uploads/numerai/optimized/1X/6dc1991c00b9daf54c16988287b67404f99e7dfc_2_290x500.png)image564×972 38.1 KB](<https://canada1.discourse-cdn.com/flex009/uploads/numerai/original/1X/6dc1991c00b9daf54c16988287b67404f99e7dfc.png> "image")

Click the “expand graph” icon to view the cumulative correlation of your model across all of the validation eras that it has submitted predictions for.
