# Updated Signals Validation Data

---

### Post #1 — **_liamhz** | 2021-02-27 19:22 UTC

[![](https://canada1.discourse-cdn.com/flex009/uploads/numerai/original/1X/416093c6ac434e152d56d16d9a4dbe420b6303ea.png)500×328 70 KB](<https://canada1.discourse-cdn.com/flex009/uploads/numerai/original/1X/416093c6ac434e152d56d16d9a4dbe420b6303ea.png>)

Signals validation data has been updated!

You can now submit signals for tickers over the past year and receive model diagnostics for them, and also download the updated dataset to train models.

Previously, the Signals validation data we provided only went up to 2020-02-28. Now, data is available up until the third most recent Friday. As of the time of writing that’s 2021-02-12. This gap is due to Signals rounds resolving two Wednesdays (11 days) after they open.

This increases the number of validation tickers from 1,873,102 to 2,118,355.

Validation data will be updated every week.
