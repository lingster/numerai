# Numerai Forum Launch Announcement

---

### Post #1 — **pschork** | 2020-03-23 20:39 UTC

Numerai is pleased to announce the launch of the [Numerai Forum](<http://forum.numer.ai>)!

Rocketchat is great for realtime community chat and good discussions take place here everyday. However, the ephemeral nature of chat and infinite scroll does not lend itself to information sharing, research or self-directed learning. With that in mind we are launching the [Numerai Forum](<http://forum.numer.ai>) where we hope to build a searchable corpus of high value long form discussions on topics relevant to the tournament, data science and other topics.

The forum can be browsed anonymously, but to participate in the Forum (ie post, comment, etc), we require that you are authenticated with a Numerai Tournament account. Unlike Rocketchat, the Forum uses Single-Sign-On (SSO) to seamlessly authenticate users against their currently logged-in Tournament account. See the [FAQ](<http://forum.numer.ai/faq>) for more details.

We have initially created [categories](<http://forum.numer.ai/categories>) for [Announcements](<http://forum.numer.ai/c/accouncements>), [the Tournament](<http://forum.numer.ai/c/tournament>), [Data Science](<http://forum.numer.ai/c/data-science>), [Erasure](<http://forum.numer.ai/c/erasure>), [Numeraire](<http://forum.numer.ai/c/numeraire>). If you think we’ve missed something, let us know in [Feedback](<http://forum.numer.ai/c/feedback>)
