# Signals Payout Update

---

### Post #1 — **ark** | 2024-02-07 22:39 UTC

MMC has returned to Signals. Payouts for Signals rounds starting on or after March 30, 2024 will use 1xFNCv4 + 2xMMC with no option to change these multipliers. This change only applies to Signals.

FAQ

When is this happening?  
March 30, 2024.

What rounds will this effect?  
Rounds starting on or after Saturday March 30, 2024.

What if I currently have 0x multipliers set?  
Your multipliers will automatically update to 1xFNCv4 + 2xMMC. If you’re uncomfortable with this, schedule stake decrease before March 30, 2024.

What happens if I’m staking and I do nothing?  
Your stake will continue, but will use 1xFNCv4 + 2xMMC starting on March 30, 2024.

Will I be able to change my multipliers?  
No.

What about the Numerai Tournament?  
This does not affect the classic tournament, which will continue to use the current multipliers. This change only applies to Signals.
