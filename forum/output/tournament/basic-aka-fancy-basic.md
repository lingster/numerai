### Post #2 — **asteeber** | 2021-04-05 14:25 UTC

That seems to be a very inefficient way to develop your model. Any visual representation of the data could just be intuited from the data itself.

For example, you could have a camera examine an image of a cluster of data points and have a neural net try to figure out the line of best fit (would take very long). Or you could just run OLS in a couple seconds.
