# Bye-Bye, V4 Data

---

### Post #1 — **svendaj** | 2024-09-28 19:08 UTC

So, from today V4 data are no more available at [Numerai platform](<https://numer.ai/data/v5.0>). Those fellows who would still like to experiment with them, will find them on Kaggle as they were downloaded last Saturday at round 839. They are available in three public datasets:

  * [numerai - V4.3 Midnight (kaggle.com)](<https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/svendaj/numerai-v4-3-midnight>)
  * [numerai data v4.2 Rain (kaggle.com)](<https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/svendaj/numerai-data-v42-rain>) and
  * [Numerai datasets V4 and V4.1 (kaggle.com)](<https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/svendaj/numerai-datasets-v4-and-v4-1>) containing both 
    * [V4 Super Massive data announced on 2021/09/09](<http://forum.numer.ai/t/super-massive-data-release-deep-dive/4053>) and
    * [V4.1 Sunshine announced on 2022/12/27](<http://forum.numer.ai/t/super-massive-data-sunshine/5977>).



BTW it is interesting to flash back on what was considered **Super Massive** 3 years ago ![:face_with_monocle:](http://forum.numer.ai/images/emoji/twitter/face_with_monocle.png?v=12).

**Latest Numerai tournament data** weekly automatically updated at the opening of Saturday round, are available in [this Kaggle public dataset](<https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/svendaj/numerai-latest-tournament-data>).
