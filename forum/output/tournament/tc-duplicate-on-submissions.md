### Post #1 — **kombuchafox** | 2022-12-28 13:58 UTC

Hey,

I am trying to understand TC performance better. If I have two identical submissions (different stake sizes) will TC for each be the same as if I submit only one?

---

### Post #2 — **wigglemuse** | 2022-12-28 14:23 UTC

Yes. Identical submissions will get same score. Two identical submissions with 200 staked on one and 100 staked on the other is exactly the same as a single submission with 300 staked on it. (The stake amounts really don’t matter either unless they are truly huge.)
