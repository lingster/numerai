### Post #2 — **themicon** | 2021-03-13 12:12 UTC

This is one of the things we’ve wanted for a while now. The team have said that they are working on it. When it will be released is not known yet.

Remember that a round is 4 weeks long and your staked NMR is “locked” into that models for the entire 4 weeks anyway.

The new stake management system would just add some new functionality, but the “locked in” period will still be the same.
