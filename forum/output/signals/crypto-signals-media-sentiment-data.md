# Crypto Signals Media Sentiment Data

---

### Post #1 — **hatcat** | 2024-10-31 11:16 UTC

Introducing a yet another source of data for **cryptocurrency sentiment** , build on hourly mass media sentiment analysis. The value, provided in the form of 24h and 7h rolling averages, is derived from processing a sample of 35 world news outlets in English, calculating sentiment and objectivity values for all contexts where the word ‘crypto’ occurs. The aim is to have an indicator on how the crypto industry is covered in MSM media. Should anyone be interested, please check it out [here](<https://textvisualization.app/cryptosentiment/>)
