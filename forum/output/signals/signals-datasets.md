Hi all!

I used to play around with Numerai signals, and while I haven’t been active recently, I’m developing a tool that might be beneficial for this community.

[subsets.io](<https://www.subsets.io/>), is an open data warehouse where you can query hundreds of datasets, such as world development indicators or Reddit engagement data, with SQL. You can also easily import them with the [Python client](<https://github.com/subsetsio/subsets-python-client>). Most tables are searchable at the row level, greatly speeding up searches for relevant data.

The engine is free to use and offers quite generous limits. I hope it’s useful, and I’d love to hear your thoughts.
