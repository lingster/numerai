# Get your (unofficial) Signals target22 here to start building your models

---

### Post #1 — **degerhan** | 2021-05-29 19:22 UTC

The [Signals target22 calculation (22day_2day target)](<https://gist.github.com/degerhan/d02d510213281f65ba80171aed7edccf>) notebook calculates the **unofficial** 22day_2day Signals target as proposed in [Longer Signals Target - A Proposal For Higher Payouts](<http://forum.numer.ai/t/longer-signals-target-a-proposal-for-higher-payouts/3357>). The goal is to start building models and receive corr20 feedback ahead of the official target22 release.

The output is `historical_targets_22.csv`, which adds a target22 column to the official historical target file. Dates/tickers where target22 cannot be computed are dropped, resulting in a reduction of target rows from approx 4.3m to 2.7m (this is with yfinance data, your mileage may vary with other data sources).

For reference, see [Decoding the signals target](<http://forum.numer.ai/t/decoding-the-signals-target/2501>) that demonstrates the official 6day_2day target is primarily (but maybe not exclusively) **the binned ranked 2day to 6day return**. We use the same method for the 22day_2day target.
